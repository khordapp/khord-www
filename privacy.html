Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Khord is a self-hosted music sharing app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information You Provide

When you register for a Khord instance, you provide an email address, a username, and a password. Your password is stored as a one-way bcrypt hash — it is never stored in plaintext.

Songs you share, votes you cast, and setlists you create are stored in the SQLite database on the Khord instance you registered with.

Information Collected Automatically

Khord instances may log standard server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) for security and debugging purposes. These logs are not shared with third parties and are retained for a short period.

The Khord Android app does not collect analytics or crash data beyond what is provided by Google Play Services on your device.

Third-Party Services

Khord integrates with the following third-party services to resolve streaming links for songs you share:

  • Spotify — track search via the Spotify Web API
  • Apple iTunes Search API — song discovery and Apple Music links
  • Deezer — track search via the public Deezer API
  • YouTube Music — track search via the YouTube Data API (when enabled)

These services are queried server-side when you share a song. No personal information is sent to these services — only song title and artist name.

Data Storage

Khord is self-hostable software. If you use a third-party Khord instance, that instance's operator controls the server and its data. Each instance may have its own data retention practices.

Your music activity (songs, votes, setlists) is stored in the database of the Khord instance you use. You can delete your account and all associated data from the instance's settings page at any time.

Children's Privacy

Khord is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to dev@khord.app.
