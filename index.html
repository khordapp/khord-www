How it works

Share once, listen anywhere.

No more "I don't use Spotify." Share a song and your friends hear it on whatever platform they love.

01

Find your song

Search by name or paste any streaming link. Doesn't matter which service you use.

02

Share it

Khord takes care of the rest — finding the link on Spotify, Apple Music, and other platforms, automatically.

03

Your friends listen their way

They open it on Spotify, Apple Music, or whatever they use. You never have to think about it.

Mixtapes

Collaborative playlists,
built together.

Mixtapes are the heart of Khord. Create an ordered playlist, share it with your community, and let anyone propose songs — all without giving up control.

I

Propose songs

Any friend can suggest a track. No write access to your mixtape needed.

II

Owner curates

Accept or dismiss proposals from your inbox. Accepted songs are added instantly; dismissed ones stay gone.

III

Arrange the perfect mix

Move songs around until the vibe is just right. Tell a story, share a moment, build it together.

IV

Every platform, one link

Each song opens on whichever streaming service each listener prefers.

Features

Everything you need.

Khord is a full social platform for music sharing, not just a link converter.

🎵

Song feed

A social feed of songs shared by people you follow, with upvoting and notes.

🔑

Decentralized

Sign in with your Bluesky account. Records live in your own identity — no passwords, no silo, no central server.

🛡️

Access control

Open your instance to everyone or keep it invite-only. Your community, your rules.

🎨

Themes

Dark, light, and chromatic color schemes. Pick one that fits your community.

Self-host

Build your own
music community.

Run a Khord instance around whatever you love — a genre, an era, a scene, a group of friends. Your instance, your vibe, your people.

Khord is fully open source and free to self-host. If you're on Unraid, install the khord and khord-indexer templates directly from Community Applications — no command line needed. For VPS or home server installs, grab the source on GitHub — a docker-compose.yml is included and you'll be up in minutes. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
